ENG-2026-0847 — acme-router-fw

4 targets · 3 architectures · 8 findings (1 PoC-backed, 2 confirmed) · generated 2026-09-08

CWE-121  Stack-based buffer overflow in cookie parser Critical

An unbounded memcpy in parse_cookie (httpd, AArch64) copies attacker-controlled cookie data into a 64-byte stack buffer, overwriting the saved link register at offset 268 and yielding full PC control. Demonstrated with a reproducible L1 crash bundle.

state: PoC-backedCVSS 9.8evidence: static-taint · AFL++ · QASan · angrghidra 11.x · sig 2026-07 · llm4decompile-6.7b-q5

CWE-416  Use-after-free of session object High

Freed session reused after session_free; confirmed by QASan under directed fuzzing. Remediation: null the handle on free and gate reuse.

state: Confirmedevidence: QASan-UAF · directed-fuzz · angr

Scope honesty

We track detectable families and their real feasibility, not a hollow "supports 900 CWEs." High/dyn families reach the analyst only after reproduction; low/manual families are labeled analyst leads, never confirmed findings.

Out of scopeWeb/app CWEs with no visible parser (XSS·SQLi·CSRF·SSRF·XXE), design/process weaknesses, and physical side-channels — recorded as "known-not-covered" so gaps show, not silence.
Managed VMsBytecode targets drop memory-safety CWEs (VM-managed) and gain deserialization / reflection / injection — the engine reports the applicable set only.
